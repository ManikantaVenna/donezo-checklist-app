Privacy Policy

Effective July 18, 2026

Donezo is a personal checklist app. We collect only the information needed to create your account, sync your checklists, and operate the service.

Information we collect

  • Your email address for passwordless sign-in.
  • The tasks, routines, projects, timezone, and reminder settings you choose to save.
  • Basic technical records needed for security and reliable delivery.

How we use information

We use your information to authenticate you, keep your Donezo data synchronized across your devices, send requested sign-in codes, and maintain the service. We do not sell your personal information or use your checklist content for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Account and checklist data are stored with Supabase. Sign-in emails are delivered through Resend. These providers process information only to operate Donezo.

Your control

You can sign out at any time. To request a copy or deletion of your account and associated data, use the contact method on the Donezo support page.

Security

Donezo uses passwordless sign-in and database row-level security so authenticated users can access only their own checklist data. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we limit access and use encrypted network connections.

Children

Donezo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as Donezo evolves. The effective date above will show the latest revision.
